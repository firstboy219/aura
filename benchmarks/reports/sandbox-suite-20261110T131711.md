### S12-english-owner
> In two sentences, what is the difference between RAM and storage?

RAM is fast, temporary memory used by your computer to hold data for apps currently running, while storage refers to hard drives or SSDs that save your files and applications permanently. RAM loses its data when the device powers off, whereas storage retains everything until you delete it.
